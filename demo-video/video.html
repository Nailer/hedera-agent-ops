Three roles, deliberately separate

A leaked key can’t raise its own limit

CONTROLLER
Sets policy
Authorises tokens, per-action and per-epoch caps. Can rotate the operator.
OPERATOR
Submits actions
The hot key. Can act inside the budget — and nothing else.
TREASURY
Holds the funds
Keeps custody. The router holds funds only inside a single transaction.
COMPROMISED OPERATOR → AgentRegistry__ExceedsPerAction reverts on-chain, before any token moves
